📌 Reservations

When a book is with someone, another member can reserve it. When it comes back, the librarian is told to keep it for them, and only they can take it.

💬 WhatsApp reminders

Tap “WhatsApp” beside a name. WhatsApp opens with the message ready; just press send. Members need a phone number.

🔍 ISBN and covers

Scan the ISBN barcode on the back of a book (it starts with 978 or 979). The title, author and cover picture come from the Open Library. Malayalam books may not be found; type them yourself.

Books with covers

Add the ISBN in a book's Edit window to show its cover everywhere.

🪪 Member ID cards

Bank-card size (8.6 × 5.4 cm), 10 cards on one A4 page, with the library name, member name, class and barcode. Print at “Actual size”.
