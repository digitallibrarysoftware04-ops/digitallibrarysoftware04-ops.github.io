FAQ

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Open the site, log in and start.

Do I need a barcode scanner?

No. The phone camera can scan barcodes. A USB barcode scanner also works on a computer, and you can always type the ID.

What happens after the free month?

Choose a plan and pay by UPI, or enter a coupon. Your books and members stay safe. Until you renew, you can see your data but cannot issue books.

Can I bring my old list of books?

Yes. Copy the columns from Excel or Google Sheets and paste them in “Add many”. Hundreds of books go in at once.

Is my data private?

Each library sees only its own books and members. Students see only their own books in self-check.
